### About Me

I am an epidemiologist and RWE data scientist who transforms messy observational healthcare data into causal evidence. My work sits at the intersection of **causal inference**, **Bayesian methods**, and **clinical research** with a focus on oncology and pregnancy safety.

---

### What I Work On

```
Causal Inference     Target trials, MSMs, G-formula, IPTW, IV
Bayesian Methods     Hierarchical models, Stan, PyMC
Therapeutic Areas    Oncology, pregnancy pharmacoepi, drug safety
Data Sources         Claims, EHR, SEER, NHANES, OMOP CDM
Languages            Python, R, SAS, SQL, Stata
```
